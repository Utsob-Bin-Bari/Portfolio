English sessions with your wife

Phase 1 judges your spoken English. Phase 3 needs you to think aloud for 45 minutes. Three short sessions a week build both. She doesn’t need to know programming. Her job is to say whether she understood you.

Schedule

  • Monday and Wednesday, 20 minutes, plus 10 minutes for your notes: 3 questions, 2 minutes each.
  • Saturday, 45 minutes: a full mock interview.
  • The break: a short think-aloud drill every day before Phase 3.

How she runs a weekday session

  1. She picks 3 questions from the bank below, each from a different group.
  2. She reads one aloud and starts a 2-minute timer on her phone.
  3. While you answer, she makes a tally mark for every “um”, “uh”, “like” or “actually”, and taps the table when you stay silent for more than 5 seconds.
  4. After each answer she scores 1 to 5 for fluency (smooth, few pauses), clarity (she understood it) and confidence (steady voice, no apologising).
  5. She tells you one thing that was good and one thing to fix. You write both down.

Saturday full mock

  • Weeks 1–4: 30 minutes of interview, about 8 questions from “About you” and “Work situations”. Then 15 minutes of feedback.
  • Weeks 5–7: 20 minutes of interview. Then a 15-minute think-aloud drill: she reads an easy LeetCode problem aloud, you solve it on paper while talking the whole time, and she taps on every silence. Then 10 minutes of feedback.
  • Weeks 8–9: she plays the Toptal recruiter: your intro, experience, weekly hours, rate, and your questions for them. Same length as the real call, about 30 minutes.

Your rules while answering

  • Answer first, details after. Keep each answer under 2 minutes.
  • Tell stories in four parts: the situation, your task, what you did, the result.
  • Short sentences and plain words.
  • Need time? Say “Let me think about that for a moment.” Then think.
  • Didn’t understand? Ask “Do you mean …?” instead of guessing.

Explain it simply

Explain as if she isn’t a developer. If she can repeat the idea back, you passed.

  1. What is React Native, and how is it different from a normal app?
  2. What happens between tapping a button and the screen changing?
  3. How does a chat app show a new message instantly?
  4. How do you keep a money app secure?
  5. Why do some apps feel slow, and how do you fix that?
  6. What is an API?
  7. What changes for a developer between Android and iOS?
  8. How does an app get into the App Store?
  9. What is “state” in an app?
  10. Explain binary search to a child.
